#### Slide 8
- Principles of column chromatography
- Peak Width (W): The magnitude of the peak base intercepted by the tangents to the inflection points of the peak.
- Retention time (tr): The time between sample injection and the appearance of a solute peak at the detector of a chromatographic column.
- Dead time or void time (tm): The time it takes for an unretained species (mobile phase) to pass through a column.
- The time at the baseline disturbance
- Retention time of uracil (reversed phase HPLC)
- Retention time of hexane (normal phase HPLC)
- Adjusted retention time (tr\): is the time solute spent in the stationary phase.
