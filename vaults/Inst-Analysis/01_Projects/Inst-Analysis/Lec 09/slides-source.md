# Fundamental Scientific Principles
$$k' = \frac{t_R - t_0}{t_0} \quad | \quad N = 16 \left(\frac{t_R}{W}\right)^2 = 5.545 \left(\frac{t_R}{W_{0.5}}\right)^2$$
$$H = \frac{L}{N} = A + \frac{B}{u} + C \cdot u \quad (\text{van Deemter Equation})$$
$$R_s = \frac{2(t_{R2} - t_{R1})}{W_1 + W_2} = \frac{\sqrt{N}}{4} \left(\frac{\alpha - 1}{\alpha}\right) \left(\frac{k_2'}{1 + k_2'}\right)$$
- Validated under standard pharmacopoeial conditions (BP/USP).
- Core constant: Chromatographic separation results from differential partition equilibria between mobile and stationary phases..

<!-- SLIDE 4: Optical / Instrumental Topography -->
# Instrumental Configuration & Topography
- **Stationary Phase**: Solid adsorbent or bonded liquid film on silica particles.
- **Mobile Phase**: Liquid or gas carrier.
- **Column Dimensions**: Length $L$, internal diameter $d_c$, particle size $d_p$.

<!-- SLIDE 5: Pharmaceutical Application & Case Study -->
# Clinical Quality Control & Assay Protocol
- **Target Drug**: TLC Separation of Aspirin, Paracetamol, and Caffeine
- **Procedure**: Spot silica gel $60\text{ F}_{254}$ plates, elute with Ethyl acetate : Methanol : Acetic acid (80:10:10), visualize under UV 254nm.
- **Acceptance Criterion**: Linear calibration $R^2 > 0.999$, precision RSD $< 2.0\%$.

<!-- SLIDE 6: Troubleshooting & Deviation Analysis -->
# Analytical Diagnostics & Troubleshooting
- Band broadening and tailing due to column overloading or extra-column dead volume.
