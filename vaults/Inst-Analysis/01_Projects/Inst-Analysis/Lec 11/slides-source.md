# Fundamental Scientific Principles
$$I = 100 \cdot \left[ n + \frac{\log(t_R') - \log(t_{R,n}')}{\log(t_{R,n+1}') - \log(t_{R,n}')} \right] \quad (\text{Kovats Retention Index})$$
$$\text{Carrier Gas Velocity }: u = \frac{L}{t_0} \quad (\text{Optimal: } \text{He} \approx 30-40\text{ cm/s}, \text{H}_2 \approx 40-60\text{ cm/s})$$
- Validated under standard pharmacopoeial conditions (BP/USP).
- Core constant: Separation in GC is governed jointly by analyte vapor pressure (boiling point) and chemical interaction with the stationary liquid phase..

<!-- SLIDE 4: Optical / Instrumental Topography -->
# Instrumental Configuration & Topography
- **Carrier Gas**: High-purity Helium or Nitrogen ($99.999\%$) with moisture/oxygen traps.
- **Injector**: Split/Splitless injector heated to $250^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Column**: Fused-silica open tubular (FSOT) capillary column ($30\text{ m} \times 0.25\text{ mm} \times 0.25\mu\text{m}$ 5% phenyl-polysiloxane).
- **Detector**: Flame Ionization Detector ($H_2 + \text{Air}$ flame).

<!-- SLIDE 5: Pharmaceutical Application & Case Study -->
# Clinical Quality Control & Assay Protocol
- **Target Drug**: Residual Solvents (Methanol, Ethanol, Acetone) in Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs)
- **Procedure**: Headspace injection ($80^\circ\text{C}$ equilibration for 30 min), oven program: $40^\circ\text{C}$ (5 min) $\to 20^\circ\text{C/min} \to 240^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Acceptance Criterion**: Linear calibration $R^2 > 0.999$, precision RSD $< 2.0\%$.

<!-- SLIDE 6: Troubleshooting & Deviation Analysis -->
# Analytical Diagnostics & Troubleshooting
- Ghost peaks due to septum bleed or sample decomposition in an excessively hot injection port.
