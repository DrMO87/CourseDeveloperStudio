# 📖 Student Take-Home Summary: GC and chromatographic theory.

## 🌟 Key Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet
$$I = 100 \cdot \left[ n + \frac{\log(t_R') - \log(t_{R,n}')}{\log(t_{R,n+1}') - \log(t_{R,n}')} \right] \quad (\text{Kovats Retention Index})$$
$$\text{Carrier Gas Velocity }: u = \frac{L}{t_0} \quad (\text{Optimal: } \text{He} \approx 30-40\text{ cm/s}, \text{H}_2 \approx 40-60\text{ cm/s})$$

- **Core Rule**: Separation in GC is governed jointly by analyte vapor pressure (boiling point) and chemical interaction with the stationary liquid phase.
- **Practical Takeaway**: Headspace injection ($80^\circ\text{C}$ equilibration for 30 min), oven program: $40^\circ\text{C}$ (5 min) $\to 20^\circ\text{C/min} \to 240^\circ\text{C}$.
