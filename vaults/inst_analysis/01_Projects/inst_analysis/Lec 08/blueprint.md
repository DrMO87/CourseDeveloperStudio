## 🧪 2. Laboratory Practical Linkage
- **Core Practical Session**: Paper chromatography of amino acids & Thin-Layer chromatography of analgesics
- **Target Skills**: Safe sample preparation, cuvette/column handling, optical alignment, and experimental data recording.

---

## ✍️ 3. Formative Practice Items (PC 206 Item Bank)
1. **Core Concept Recall**: State the fundamental governing principle of Paper Chromatography (PC) & Thin-Layer Chromatography (TLC).
2. **Quantitative Application**: Apply the governing mathematical law ($$R_f = \frac{\text{Distance traveled by solute}}{\text{Distance traveled by solvent front}} \quad | \quad R_{st} = \frac{\text{Distance solute}}{\text{Distance standard solute}}$$) to solve for unknown analyte parameters.
