- 3- Mass transfer
- As the solute passes through the column it moves between the mobile phase and the stationary phase and we call this movement between phases mass transfer.
- Band broadening occurs if the solute’s movement within the mobile phase or within the stationary phase is not fast enough to maintain an equilibrium partitioning of solute between the two phases.
