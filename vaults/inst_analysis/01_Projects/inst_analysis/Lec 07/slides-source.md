- 1. Mobile phase reservoirs
- A modern HPLC apparatus is equipped with one or more glass reservoirs each of which contain 500 ml or more of the mobile phase.
- the reservoirs contain a filtration system for filtering dust and particulate matters from the solvent to prevent these particles from damaging:
- the pumps
- injection valves
- blocking the column.

- 1. Mobile phase reservoirs
- The elution order of solutes in HPLC is governed by polarity.
- In reversed-phase HPLC (Stationary phase is non-polar & mobile phase is polar)
- the order of elution is more polar solutes eluting first.
- Increasing the polarity of the mobile phase leads to longer retention times.
- Shorter retention times require a mobile phase of lower polarity.
- The elution order is reversed in normal-phase HPLC

- 2. Pump
- Pumps work on two modes:
- Isocratic: Constant eluent composition
- Gradient: Gradually varying eluent composition
- e.g. water (10%, 20% and 30%), and methanol (90%, 80% and 70%).
